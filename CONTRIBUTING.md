# Contributing

## Current status

AetherHarness is a **paused boundary investigation**, not an active project.
Phase 0 is NOT AUTHORIZED, and implementation is NOT AUTHORIZED.

Contributions that **advance the investigation** are welcome. Contributions that
**implement** AetherHarness are not — they are premature.

## What counts as a contribution

- New external literature on synchronization, composition, or responsibility
  boundaries that bears on H1–H5.
- New Aether papers that change the overlap picture (especially AetherHuman and
  AetherGovern).
- Evidence that H1–H5 are architectural (separate responsibilities) or
  operational (uses of existing dimensions).
- Evidence that the synchronization remainder does or does not exist.
- Review and sign-off of RESEARCH/BOUNDARY_INVESTIGATION.md.

## What does NOT count

- Implementations, prototypes, or code.
- Architectural proposals (Phase 0 is not authorized).
- Claims that AetherHarness is an independent layer (undetermined).
- Roadmaps.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the evidence or literature.
2. Reference the specific section of RESEARCH/BOUNDARY_INVESTIGATION.md, HRT-01, or
   HRT-02 that the contribution bears on.
3. Do not submit pull requests that implement anything.

## Methodological constraints

This project follows the falsification-first methodology of the Aether family.
All claims must be stated in falsifiable form. No claim may be made that Phase 0
has been reached, that the synchronization remainder exists, or that
AetherHarness is independent, until the evidence supports it.
