
Architectural Hypothesis

Status: UNFORMULATED
Reason: Phase 0 is NOT AUTHORIZED.

---

Why this file exists

AetherHarness is a boundary investigation. The question it investigates is:

Is the composition of existing Aether dimensions sufficient to guarantee
synchronization across responsibilities, or does an architectural remainder
survive composition?

An architectural hypothesis would be a positive claim about what AetherHarness
is — its primitives, its invariants, its composition rules, its interface
with existing Aether dimensions.

No such hypothesis has been formulated, and none is authorized.

---

Preconditions for Phase 0

Phase 0 (Architectural Hypothesis) may only be authorized if:

1. The boundary investigation is reviewed and signed
   (currently: DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED, NOT SIGNED).
2. HRT-01 establishes whether H1–H5 are architectural responsibilities or
   operational uses of existing Aether dimensions
   (currently: PENDING).
3. HRT-02 establishes complete synchronization coverage or establishes
   that it cannot be established
   (currently: complete coverage NOT IDENTIFIED).
4. Compositional reconstruction is sufficient
   (currently: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE).
5. The synchronization remainder is established
   (currently: CANDIDATE).
6. AetherHarness independence is established
   (currently: UNDETERMINED).

None of these preconditions is currently satisfied.
